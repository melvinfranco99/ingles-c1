Gramática para el C1 Advanced

En el C1 se da por supuesta toda la gramática de B2. Aquí tienes lo que marca la diferencia en el nivel C1: estructuras que aparecen constantemente en las Partes 2 y 4 del Use of English y que, bien usadas en Writing y Speaking, suben la nota de Language. Cada tema tiene explicación en español, ejemplos y un ejercicio con corrección.

1. Tiempos verbales: matices avanzados

EstructuraUsoEjemplo
Present perfect continuousActividad reciente con resultados visibles; énfasis en la duraciónYou look exhausted. Have you been running?
Past perfect continuousActividad prolongada antes de otro momento pasadoShe had been working there for a decade when the company collapsed.
Future perfect (continuous)Algo terminado / en curso antes de un momento futuroBy June, I will have been living here for ten years.
Verbos de estado en continuoCambio de significadoI think so (opinión) / I’m thinking about it (considerar). He is being rude (comportamiento temporal).
Pasado «de distancia»Peticiones más cortesesI was wondering if you could help me. I wanted to ask you something.

2. Formas de futuro avanzadas

ExpresiónSignificadoEjemplo
be about to / be on the point of + -ing / be on the verge of + -ingA punto deThe company is on the verge of collapsing.
be bound to / be sure toSeguro que (predicción firme)Prices are bound to rise.
be due toPrevisto (horario oficial)The report is due to be published in May.
be set toVa a (lenguaje periodístico)Unemployment is set to fall.
be to + inf.Planes oficiales / órdenesThe minister is to visit Madrid. You are not to leave.
Futuro en el pasado: was going to, would, was about toPlanes que no se cumplieronI was going to call you, but I forgot. I was about to leave when he arrived.

3. Condicionales mixtos e invertidos

Mixtos: combinan tiempos distintos. If I had taken the job, I would be living in London now (pasado → presente). If I weren’t so shy, I would have spoken to her (rasgo permanente → pasado).

Invertidos (formales, sin if):

NormalInvertido
If you should need help…Should you need help, call me.
If I were in your position…Were I in your position, I’d accept.
If I had known…Had I known, I would have come.
If it weren’t for / hadn’t been for…Were it not for / Had it not been for your help…

Otras: but for + nombre, otherwise, provided / providing (that), as long as, on condition that, supposing, unless.

4. Wish, if only, would rather, as if

EstructuraUsoEjemplo
wish / if only + pasadoDeseo sobre el presenteI wish I knew the answer.
wish / if only + past perfectArrepentimiento sobre el pasadoIf only I had listened.
wish + wouldQueja sobre el comportamiento de otroI wish you would stop complaining.
would rather / would sooner + sujeto + pasadoPreferencia sobre otra personaI’d rather you didn’t tell her.
it’s (high/about) time + sujeto + pasadoYa va siendo horaIt’s high time we left.
as if / as though + pasado (irreal)Como siHe talks as if he owned the place.

5. Pasiva avanzada y estructuras impersonales

EstructuraEjemplo
It is said / thought / believed / reported that…It is thought that the fire started in the kitchen.
Sujeto + is said to + inf. / to have + participioThe fire is thought to have started in the kitchen.
There is said to be…There are believed to be 200 survivors.
Pasiva con gerundio / infinitivoI hate being interrupted. She expects to be promoted.
Causativo: have / get something doneWe had the roof repaired.
have sb do / get sb to doI got my brother to help me.
need + -ing (= need to be done)The windows need cleaning.

6. Modales en pasado

ModalSignificadoEjemplo
must have + part.Deducción seguraShe must have left early.
can’t / couldn’t have + part.ImposibleHe can’t have seen us.
may / might / could have + part.PosibilidadThey might have got lost.
should / ought to have + part.Crítica, arrepentimientoYou should have warned me.
needn’t have + part.Se hizo, pero no era necesarioYou needn’t have bought a present.
didn’t need to + inf.No era necesario (normalmente no se hizo)I didn’t need to queue.

could have + part.Posibilidad no aprovechadaYou could have told me!

7. Inversión tras expresiones negativas

Cuando una expresión negativa o restrictiva va al principio, el orden cambia como en una pregunta (auxiliar + sujeto). Es muy típico de la Parte 4 y da un toque formal en el Writing.

ExpresiónEjemplo
Never / Rarely / SeldomRarely do we see such talent.
Hardly / Scarcely … when · No sooner … thanNo sooner had I arrived than it started to rain.
Not until / Only when / Only afterNot until later did I realise my mistake.
Under no circumstances / On no account / In no wayUnder no circumstances should you open the door.
LittleLittle did she know what awaited her.
Not only … but alsoNot only did he apologise, but he also paid for the damage.
So / Such … thatSo great was the demand that tickets sold out.

8. Cleft sentences (frases escindidas)

Sirven para destacar una parte de la frase.

TipoEjemplo
It is/was … that/who …It was Laura who found the solution.
What … is/was …What annoys me is his attitude.
All … is/was …All I want is some peace.
The thing / reason / place … is …The reason why I left was the salary.
What happened was (that) …What happened was that the car broke down.
What + do + is/was + inf.What she did was (to) call the police.

9. Relativos avanzados y oraciones de participio

  • Preposición + which / whom (formal): the person to whom I spoke, the extent to which …
  • Cuantificador + of which / of whom: He has three sisters, all of whom are doctors.
  • whereby (= by which): a system whereby employees share profits.
  • which referido a toda la frase: He didn’t apologise, which annoyed me.
  • Oraciones de participio: Having finished the report, she went home. · Not knowing what to say, I kept quiet. · The man sitting next to me… · The products made in China…

+ -ingdeny, admit, suggest, recommendHe denied stealing it.

+ obj. + prep. + -ingaccuse of, blame for, congratulate on, criticise forThey blamed him for losing the match.

13. Conectores y cohesión

FunciónConectores de nivel C1
Contrastenevertheless, nonetheless, even so, whereas, while, albeit, notwithstanding, despite / in spite of (the fact that)
Concesiónadmittedly, granted, although, much as I like…, however hard he tries…
Consecuenciaconsequently, hence, thus, therefore, as a result, thereby
Finalidadso as (not) to, in order (not) to, so that, lest (formal)
Adiciónmoreover, furthermore, what is more, not to mention, let alone
Condiciónprovided that, on condition that, assuming that, in the event of
Cohesión no es solo conectores: usa referencias (this trend, such measures, the former / the latter) y sinónimos para no repetir palabras.

14. Énfasis, sustitución y elipsis

  • do / does / did enfático: I did tell you! · She does look tired.
  • so / neither / nor + auxiliar: He can’t swim and neither can I.
  • so / not para sustituir frases: I hope so. I’m afraid not. If so, … If not, …
  • one / ones: I prefer the red one.
  • Elipsis con to: I’d like to, but I can’t. · You don’t have to come if you don’t want to.
  • Intensificadores: by far the best, the very first, nowhere near as good, not in the least, whatsoever.
